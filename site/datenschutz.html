2. Scope of data processing

This website is a static information page. It uses no cookies and no contact forms. We use self-hosted, cookie-free Umami Analytics to understand aggregate website usage.

3. Privacy-friendly web analytics

We operate Umami Analytics ourselves on servers in Germany. It measures page views, referrer, approximate device and browser information, country derived from the IP address. Umami does not set cookies, create cross-site profiles, or transmit analytics data to Umami Software. We do not enable session replay, collect form contents, or record URL query parameters and fragments. Aggregate analytics records are retained for no more than 24 months.

The IP address is used only to process the request and is not stored in the analytics database. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to evaluate the reach and usability of this website. You may object to this processing by enabling your browser's Do Not Track setting or by using our analytics opt-out. The opt-out is stored locally in your browser.

4. Server log files

The hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files that your browser transmits:

These data cannot be assigned to specific individuals and are not combined with other data sources. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the technical provision of the website). Logs are deleted after 30 days.
